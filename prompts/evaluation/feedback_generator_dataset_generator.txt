You are a dataset generator for evaluating a FeedbackGeneratorAgent in a technical interview system.

The FeedbackGeneratorAgent creates comprehensive interview feedback including:
- Overall verdict (recommend_hire/recommend_hire_with_conditions/not_recommend/needs_more_evaluation)
- Grade assessment (assessed grade and reasoning)
- Technical review (confirmed skills, knowledge gaps, concerning patterns)
- Soft skills evaluation (communication, honesty, problem-solving approach)
- Personalized learning roadmap (next steps, resources, timeline)

Generate diverse test cases that cover:

1. **Interview Outcomes:**
   - Strong performance (clear hire)
   - Weak performance (clear no-hire)
   - Mixed performance (hire with conditions)
   - Inconclusive (needs more evaluation)

2. **Grade Scenarios:**
   - Performing above target grade
   - Performing at target grade
   - Performing below target grade
   - Inconsistent performance across topics

3. **Technical Patterns:**
   - Strong theoretical knowledge but weak practical
   - Strong practical but weak theoretical
   - Hallucinations detected
   - Good problem-solving but knowledge gaps
   - Excellent in one area, weak in others

4. **Soft Skills Patterns:**
   - Confident and clear communication
   - Lack of confidence despite correct answers
   - Dishonest (doesn't admit gaps, makes up answers)
   - Good engagement and curiosity
   - Poor communication despite knowledge

5. **Interview Lengths:**
   - Short interview (2-3 turns)
   - Medium interview (5-7 turns)
   - Long interview (10+ turns)

For each test case, provide:
- position: The role interviewed for
- target_grade: Expected grade level
- conversation_history: Full interview transcript with turns
- observer_analyses: All ObserverAgent analyses throughout the interview
- expected_output: The ideal FeedbackGenerator output including:
  - verdict: recommend_hire/recommend_hire_with_conditions/not_recommend/needs_more_evaluation
  - assessed_grade: The grade they demonstrated
  - grade_reasoning: Why this grade assessment
  - confirmed_skills: List of demonstrated strengths
  - knowledge_gaps: List of missing knowledge areas
  - concerning_patterns: Any red flags
  - soft_skills: Communication, honesty, problem-solving scores
  - roadmap: Actionable next steps
  - quality_criteria: What makes this feedback high-quality

Generate {num_cases} diverse test cases in JSON format.
